# Grand Project Summary

The Helix AI orchestrator landing page is essentially feature-complete across the scaffold and section layers. Foundations are locked in: Next.js 14 (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind scaffold with the Helix brand theme in `app/globals.css` and `lib/brand.ts`, reusable atoms in `components/ui/`, the navbar/footer shells in `components/layout/`, and Vercel-ready config (`vercel.json`, `.vercelignore`, `.gitignore`). SEO wiring is done via `app/sitemap.ts`, `app/robots.ts`, `app/opengraph-image.tsx`, `app/icon.tsx`, `app/apple-icon.tsx`, plus `public/og/**` and `public/favicon.ico`. Every marketing section ships with brand-aware copy and assets: Hero (`components/Hero.tsx`, `components/landing/hero/**`), HowItWorks (`components/HowItWorks.tsx`, `components/landing/how-it-works/**`), Features (`components/Features.tsx`, `components/landing/features/**`), Personas (`components/Personas.tsx`, `components/landing/personas/**`), SocialProof (`components/SocialProof.tsx`, `components/social-proof/**`, `public/logos/**`), FAQ accordions (`components/sections/FAQ.tsx`, `components/FAQ.tsx`, `components/faq/**`), CTA blocks (`components/sections/CTA.tsx`, `components/CTA.tsx`, `components/landing/cta/**`), and Footer shells (`components/Footer.tsx`, `components/footer/**`, `components/landing/footer/**`) with FAQ/CTA copy in `content/faq.json` and `content/cta.json`. The previously blocking Hero import path issue is resolved via the canonical `components/sections/` tree. Three cards remain open: USER-303000 (final `app/page.tsx` + `components/landing/Sections.tsx` assembly and polish), USER-285000 (scaffold README), and USER-94000 (Footer with Vercel SEO polish). Final milestone is closing those three cards and shipping a clean Vercel deploy.
