# Grand Project Summary

The Helix landing page is a separate, Vercel-deployable Next.js 14 (App Router) + TypeScript site that markets the orchestrator to every persona on the reference app and GitHub repo. Foundations are locked in: `app/globals.css` plus `lib/theme.ts` / `lib/brand.ts` provide the polished gradient-and-grid background, typography (Inter/JetBrains Mono via `next/font`), and design tokens every section consumes. All section components are shipped and section-complete — the Features grid and 3-step How-It-Works live at `components/sections/Features.tsx`, `components/sections/FeatureCard.tsx`, and `components/sections/HowItWorks.tsx`; social proof, FAQ accordion, personas, and final CTA bands sit under `components/sections/social-proof/**`, `components/sections/faq/**`, `components/sections/Personas.tsx`, and `components/sections/CTASection.tsx`; the top-of-page Hero, features strip, and personas block are at `components/sections/hero/**`, `components/sections/features-strip/**`, and `components/sections/personas/**`; and the Footer + Final CTA banner are at `components/Footer.tsx` and `components/FinalCTA.tsx`, wired into `app/layout.tsx`. Reference-vs-live copy has been corrected across `app/**` and `components/**` so helix-ai-orchestrator.vercel.app is always labeled a reference site, and Vercel deploy config + SEO (`vercel.json`, `public/favicon.ico`, `public/og.png`, `src/app/layout.tsx.metadata.ts`, `src/app/opengraph-image.tsx`, `src/app/icon.tsx`) are in place. Work now converges on a few parallel tracks — page assembly/footer+CTA+README wiring (`app/page.tsx`, `app/layout.tsx`, `components/landing/Sections.tsx`, `README.md`), the Hero+personas section consolidation, and final Vercel integration polish — after which the site is ready for marketing launch.
